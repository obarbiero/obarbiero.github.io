blurb: We develop three kinds of firm-level exposure to tariffs for US public companies. We compare exposures and effects of the 2018 and 2025 tariff episodes.
abstract: "We construct firm-level exposure to the 2018 and 2025 US tariffs through own imports, suppliers’ imports, and foreign retaliation, and estimate their joint effects using quarterly financial statements. Exposure is widespread and travels down supply chains, reaching firms that import little themselves. In 2025, cost exposure reduced sales and cost of goods sold equally within three quarters, by about 1.2 percent for the average direct importer and 0.5 percent through suppliers. Firms at least partly passed tariffs on to prices and shrank production rather than cutting margins. Retaliation instead compressed gross margins. The 2018 effects are small."
